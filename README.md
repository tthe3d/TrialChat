# 💬 TrialChat

> **🚧 Status:** In development

**TrialChat** is a World of Warcraft add-on that lets nearby players—including players on trial accounts—chat with each other, as long as they have the add-on installed.

## ✨ Features

- 📍 **Proximity chat** using friendly nameplates, saved listeners, and group members.
- 💭 **Chat bubbles** for nearby and group messages, including group-colored bubbles.
- 👥 **Groups** with invites, member management, group icons, saved history, offline reconnection, and history synchronization.
- 🗂️ **Chat history** for up to 1,000 nearby messages and 2,000 group messages.
- 🪟 **Minimal chat view** with unread notices, scrolling, drag-and-resize support, and typing shortcuts.
- 🔗 **Interactive links** for webpages, items, quests, and Musician songs.
- 🔔 **Sound notifications and sound emotes**, plus searchable emoji and emote pickers.
- 🔄 **Chat echoes** to `/s` or `/p`, so players without TrialChat can still see nearby messages. Emotes are not echoed.

## 🎮 How to Use

### Open TrialChat
- Type `/tc` in chat, click the minimap button, or use the keybind available in the game's keybind menu.

### Customize your chat
- Open **Settings** from the minimap menu or click the ⚙️ gear button.
- Configure chat bubbles, nearby-chat echoes, minimized chat, and sound emotes.

### Send emotes and sound emotes
- Use regular WoW emotes, such as `/wave`.
- Click the ☺️ smiley button to browse and insert emoji and emotes.
- Type `//name` to play a sound emote, such as `//fart` or `//meow`.

For instructions on adding sounds, see [Media/SoundEmotes/README.txt](Media/SoundEmotes/README.txt).

### Chat with players and groups
- Create or join groups, invite players, select group icons, manage members, and synchronize group history from the group menus.
- Right-click a nearby player or group member to start a TrialChat whisper. Whispers are kept separate for each character.
- Right-click a message to reply to it or copy its text.
